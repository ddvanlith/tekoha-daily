1 BIG THING: a lot in Encarnación gave up

Somewhere in Encarnación an owner spent more than a year answering the same question and yesterday changed the answer.

A terreno there, published 465 days, came down Gs 200,000,000, from Gs 850,000,000 to Gs 650,000,000, a 23.5% cut to the asking price and the deepest on our board in 26 hours, per the RE/MAX feed.

Why it matters: a deep cut on an old listing is the closest thing this market has to a seller telling you they are ready, and nobody else re-reads the same listing every day to catch it.

  • The move in dollars: about $141,900 to $108,500 at Gs 5,992, roughly $33,400 off. Asking price, not a close.
  • The company it keeps: six of seven sale cuts are land. The one house is a 209m2 casa in Filadelfia, down 6.4% to about $147,400, roughly $705 per built m2.
  • The other direction: five increases in the same window, and five rent cuts.
  • Where it came from: RE/MAX, every row. uProp and InfoCasas logged no qualifying sale cuts. One network's day.

PARA VOS

El descuento es chico y la espera es larga. Lo segundo es lo que te cuesta plata.

Sobre 1,061 ventas con cierre reportado por la red RE/MAX, la mediana del descuento contra el último precio publicado es 3.0%. Un cuarto cerró al precio pedido o por encima.

Por qué importa: si estás por publicar, el precio que vas a defender se mueve poco. Los meses que vas a sostenerlo dependen del tipo de inmueble.

En números (avisos activos de venta, días desde la primera publicación):

  • Terreno: la mitad lleva 134 días o más, un cuarto pasa los 411. n=5,838.
  • Dúplex: la mitad lleva 215, un cuarto pasa los 470. n=273.
  • Casa: la mitad lleva 77, un cuarto pasa los 214. n=4,479.
  • Departamento: la mitad lleva 77, un cuarto pasa los 215. n=2,897.

El dato raro: en los ocho cierres más recientes en la misma moneda que publicamos, la antigüedad no explicó el descuento. Cambyretá, terreno, 2,139 días: cerró al pedido, Gs 260,000,000. Ciudad del Este, terreno, 1 día: al pedido, Gs 600,000,000. Lambaré, local, 516 días: 6.0% abajo, el mayor de la semana. n=8, pregunta abierta.

Qué sigue: mediana por barrio, cuando llegue la geocodificación.

En resumen: antes de bajar, mirá cuántos días llevás contra la mediana de tu tipo. Si estás por debajo, todavía no es el precio. Es el calendario.

THE UNDERWRITE

Everyone who wires money here has been told the same story about negotiating, and the closes tell a shorter one. Half of the sales the broker panel reports close within 3.0% of the terminal asking price and a quarter close at or above it, so the cost of a position is measured in days held, not points conceded.

  • Ask-to-close, sales: P50 -3.0%. n=1,061, broker-reported, RE/MAX panel, terminal ask, same-currency only.
  • Distribution: P25 -8.6% / P75 0.0%. Same set.
  • Closes, 7 days: 13 sales, median $84,092. Broker panel.
  • Staleness, active sale stock: terreno P50 134 days / P75 411. n=5,838, full-universe sources.
  • vs 1 year ago: not available. Series begins 2026-08.

By the numbers: a quarter of closes at or above ask; a quarter at -8.6% or worse. The 8.6-point interquartile range is the input.

Between the lines: land carries. Half of active terreno listings are 134 days old and a quarter are past 411; duplex runs 215 and 470. Underwrite time to liquidity, not negotiating room.

Reality check: the data cannot tell you whether age predicts discount. Among the eight most recent same-currency closes, a 2,139-day lot in Cambyretá and a 1-day lot in Ciudad del Este both closed at full ask. n=8. A question, not a finding.

What's next: barrio-level staleness with geocoding. Until then, read this as a country-level table.

LIVING HERE

Five landlords blinked yesterday, and none of them called their tenants to say so.

That is the number of rent cuts our system caught in 26 hours.

For context: asking rents here do not move on a calendar. A landlord holds the number until the unit has sat empty long enough to sting, then cuts once. I watched my own landlord do it across the hall, back when I was renting, and only found out because the listing changed.

You do not have to be moving for this to reach your rent.

  • The closed median is a floor, not your barrio. 19 rentals closed in the past week at a median of $634 a month, per our broker panel, country-wide, every size.
  • Old listings answer the phone. Half of active departamentos (a departamento is a flat in a building; the expensas, the monthly building charge, arrive on top of the rent) have been published 77 days, a quarter past 215.
  • The sale cuts are land. Six of seven on today's board are terrenos (a terreno is a bare plot, sold with nothing on it), including one in Areguá from about $17,500 to $15,000, asking.

What it means for you: at renewal, say this before you say a number: "How long has the empty unit downstairs been listed?" Days published is the leverage. The $634 is the floor you check the answer against.

Watch for: whether the cuts keep coming. Five in a day is five owners out of patience at once. A week of them is a repricing.

1 PROPERTY THING

Somebody in Cambyretá listed a terreno on 26 October 2020 and asked Gs 260,000,000. Then they waited 2,139 days without touching the price. I cannot manage that in a bank queue. It closed on Friday at full ask, per the broker panel. The same week a lot in Ciudad del Este closed at its full asking price of Gs 600,000,000 after one day. One of them had a plan. We are not guessing which.

Un cierre a 2,139 días y otro a 1 día, los dos al precio pedido. Alguien tuvo paciencia y alguien tuvo suerte. No decimos quién.
