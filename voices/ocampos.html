1 BIG THING: the Encarnación seller who stopped waiting

I have sat across from this seller. Not this one, the type: more than a year of showings, then one morning the price moves in one step instead of ten.

A terreno in Encarnación published for 465 days came down Gs 200,000,000 yesterday, from Gs 850,000,000 to Gs 650,000,000, a 23.5% cut to the asking price and the deepest on our board in 26 hours, per the RE/MAX feed.

Why it matters: depth plus age is the closest thing this market has to a seller who has decided. Nobody else re-reads the same aviso every day.

  • The move: about $141,900 down to $108,500 at Gs 5,992, roughly $33,400 off. Asking, not closed.
  • The pattern: six of seven sale cuts on the board are terreno. The exception is a 209m2 casa in Filadelfia, down 6.4% to about $147,400, roughly $705 per built m2.
  • The counterweight: five increases and five rent cuts in the same window.
  • Sourcing: every row is RE/MAX; uProp and InfoCasas recorded no qualifying sale cuts. One network's day.

PARA VOS

Veintiocho años escuchando que acá todo se regatea. Los cierres dicen otra cosa.

Sobre 1,061 ventas con cierre reportado por la red RE/MAX, la mediana del descuento contra el último precio publicado es 3.0%. Un cuarto cerró al precio pedido o por encima. El regateo es corto. Lo que no es corto es la espera.

Por qué importa: si estás por publicar, el comprador no te va a pelear el precio. Te va a hacer esperar, y la espera tiene mediana por tipo de inmueble.

En números (avisos activos de venta, días desde la primera publicación):

  • Terreno: la mitad lleva 134 días o más; un cuarto pasa los 411. n=5,838.
  • Dúplex: la mitad lleva 215 días; un cuarto pasa los 470. n=273.
  • Casa: la mitad lleva 77 días; un cuarto pasa los 214. n=4,479.
  • Departamento: la mitad lleva 77 días; un cuarto pasa los 215. n=2,897.

El dato raro: en los ocho cierres más recientes en la misma moneda que publicamos, la antigüedad no explicó nada. Un terreno en Cambyretá con 2,139 días cerró al pedido, Gs 260,000,000. Otro en Ciudad del Este con 1 día cerró al pedido, Gs 600,000,000. El mayor descuento fue un local en Lambaré con 516 días: 6.0% abajo. n=8. Lo anoto como pregunta.

Qué sigue: la mediana por barrio llega con la geocodificación. Hasta entonces, compará contra tu tipo, no contra el vecino.

En resumen: yo, en tu lugar, antes de bajar miraría cuántos días llevás publicado contra la mediana de tu tipo. Si todavía no llegaste, el mercado todavía no te contestó.

THE UNDERWRITE

Foreign buyers arrive at my desk ready to negotiate, and the closes say the negotiation is short: half of the sales the broker panel reports close within 3.0% of the terminal asking price, a quarter close at or above it, and what is long is the wait.

  • Ask-to-close, sales: P50 -3.0%. n=1,061, broker-reported, RE/MAX network, terminal ask, same-currency only.
  • Distribution: P25 -8.6% / P75 0.0%. Same set.
  • Closes, 7 days: 13 sales, median $84,092. Broker panel.
  • Staleness, active sale stock: terreno P50 134 days / P75 411. n=5,838, full-universe sources.
  • vs 1 year ago: not available. Series begins 2026-08.

By the numbers: a quarter of closes at or above asking; a quarter at -8.6% or worse. The 8.6-point interquartile range is the underwriting input, not the median.

Between the lines: a terreno position is a carry position: half of active land listings are 134 days old, the top quartile is past 411, and duplex runs 215 and 470. Underwrite the days.

Reality check: the eight most recent same-currency closes say age did not predict discount. A Cambyretá lot published 2,139 days closed at full ask; a Ciudad del Este lot published one day did the same. n=8. I have met both kinds of seller; the data cannot yet tell them apart.

What's next: barrio-level staleness once geocoding lands. Do not underwrite a barrio off this table.

LIVING HERE

Your landlord has a number in mind, and it is older than you think.

Five rent cuts came through our system in 26 hours.

For context: an owner here does not reprice a rental on a schedule. The asking rent holds until the unit has sat empty long enough to hurt, then drops in one step. Five owners reaching that point on one day is a cluster.

You do not have to be moving for this to reach your rent.

  • The closed median is a floor, not your barrio. 19 rentals closed in the past week at a median of $634 a month, per our broker panel, country-wide, every size.
  • Old listings answer the phone. Half of active departamentos (a departamento is a flat in a building; expensas are the monthly building charge on top) have been published 77 days, a quarter past 215.
  • The sale cuts are land. Six of seven on today's board are terrenos (a terreno is a bare plot, sold with nothing on it), including one in Areguá from about $17,500 to $15,000, asking.

What it means for you: at renewal, walk the building first. Ask the portero (the doorman, who knows everything) how long the empty unit has been empty, then ask your landlord for the number. Days published is the argument.

Watch for: whether the cuts keep coming. One day of cuts is five owners. A week of them is a price.

1 PROPERTY THING

In Cambyretá somebody published a terreno on 26 October 2020 at Gs 260,000,000. It closed on Friday at Gs 260,000,000, 2,139 days later, per the broker panel. The same week a lot in Ciudad del Este closed at its full asking price of Gs 600,000,000 after one day. I have known sellers like the first one: usually right about the price, wrong about the calendar.

Uno esperó 2,139 días y el otro un día, los dos al precio pedido. En este oficio eso se llama paciencia o suerte, según quién cuente.
