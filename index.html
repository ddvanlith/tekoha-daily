Method notes

Asking prices except where marked as closes. Active means listed and seen by our capture in the last 7 days (14 for InfoCasas), counted once per property: listings entity resolution joined into one property, and listings a pending review pair links, count once. Century 21, a partial crawl with unpriced closes, counts nowhere. Dollars at SET's mid rate. Stock age uses sources captured whole whose publish dates are not one bulk load. Closes are agent-reported from one network, not a registry; identical records from one office count once (xN). A price step of -90% or +900% or more, or one that returns the ask to a price it held in the previous 7 days, is a correction and is not counted. Since 23 Sep 2026 these are the dashboard's definitions, number for number. No accuracy percentage is published; the discount figures above are measured, not modeled.
