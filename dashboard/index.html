    Method notes

    Asking and closed are different things

    Every figure here is either an asking price, what a seller advertises today, or a closed price, what a property actually sold or rented for. Each carries its tag and they are never combined in one number. An asking price is a claim, not a value: a listing can sit at its ask for a year.

    What a close is here

    Paraguay has no public sold-price registry and no MLS. Our closes are broker-reported: RE/MAX listings marked sold or rented, with the price and date the broker published ( sales and rentals with a price since 2024). That is one franchise network, not the market. Century 21 closed records carry the final ask but no sold price, so they are held out of every closed figure. Earlier weeks are thinner because the feed only shows the closed listings RE/MAX still indexes, so a rising count across years is coverage, not activity.

    Ask-to-close

    Each broker-reported sale is compared with its last asking price before it closed. Only same-currency sales count: a guaraní close against a dollar ask measures the broker's exchange rate, not the negotiation. Ratios outside 0.4 to 1.6 are dropped as entry errors. A market needs 30 such closes before a spread is shown.

    Minimum samples

    Medians and percentiles need 20 listings or closes, the ask-to-close spread needs 30 and a map cell needs 5. Below that the page shows the n and no figure. Counts are always shown.

    Currency

    Listings keep the currency they were advertised in. USD figures convert guaraní asks at one rate, the SET mid rate of Gs per USD dated . Closes convert at the SET mid rate of their own sale date, so a 2024 close is not repriced at today's rate.

    Markets

    A city gets its own page once it has 100 active sale listings; smaller towns count in their department. A listing takes its department and city from its own labels where the source labels reliably (RE/MAX, InfoCasas, Coldwell Banker, Century 21). uProp's city field is misaligned (listings in central Asunción arrive labeled as a distant town), so uProp and unlabeled listings take the majority label of their five nearest labeled listings: within 3 km for the city, 25 km for the department. Today, active listings: own label, nearest labeled listings, department only, not placed.

    Guards

    A cut deeper than 90% or a rise above 900% is an entry correction, not a repricing, and is not counted. USD per m2 uses built area only, for casa, departamento, duplex, oficina and local, 40 to 800 m2 and USD 30,000 to 2,000,000. Asking rents outside USD 50 to 15,000 a month are dropped. New to market means first seen by us within 7 days of the portal's own publish date, so a backfill never reads as new stock. Identical cuts posted by several brokers show once, marked xN.

    Stock age

    Days since the portal's own publish date, active sale listings, from sources we capture whole every day (). uProp is left out: of its active sale listings carry a publish date inside one week of June 2026, a bulk load, so its dates measure the load and not the listing.

    The map

    Cells are colored by the median asking USD per built m2 of the chosen kind: 0.01 degrees (about 1 km) when zoomed in, 0.05 and 0.25 degrees further out, each computed from its own listings. Portals place a listing with a withheld address on a city or barrio center; active listings sit on such shared points (100 or more from one source at identical coordinates) and stay off the map while still counting in their market. Tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.

    What this data cannot tell you

    What a property is worth. Asking prices are claims and our closes come from one network. The page publishes no forecasts, estimates or yields: a median rent divided by a median price mixes different properties and is not a yield. InfoCasas carries an ask on of its active listings today, while prices are re-captured after a September 2026 parser fix, so asking medians under-weight InfoCasas until that completes. Bank base prices are the banks' own figures, net of 10% IVA where the bank includes it; implausible ones are flagged, not corrected.
