Method notes

Asking prices except where marked as closes. Active means listed and seen by our capture in the last 7 days (14 for InfoCasas); Century 21, a partial crawl with unpriced closes, counts nowhere. Dollars at SET's mid rate. Stock age uses sources captured whole whose publish dates are not one bulk load. Closes are agent-reported from one network, not a registry; identical records from one office count once (xN). Price-cut percentages exclude data artifacts. Since 23 Sep 2026 these are the dashboard's definitions, number for number. No accuracy percentage is published; the discount figures above are measured, not modeled.
